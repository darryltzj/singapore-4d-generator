About the data and methods
  • Results come from 4dmoon.com's Singapore 4D archive and are refreshed automatically after each draw (see the repository README).
  • Draws #5350 (9 Jul 2025) and #5362–5367 (6–17 Aug 2025) are missing from that archive. No draws were held from April to June 2020.
  • Each of the 23 prizes is drawn separately, so the same number can win twice in one draw. That happened in about 39 draws, which is what chance predicts.
  • Big bet payouts per $1: 1st $2,000, 2nd $1,000, 3rd $490, Starter $250, Consolation $60.
  • "Chance in the next draw" for a Big bet = 1 − (9,999/10,000)23 ≈ 0.23%.
